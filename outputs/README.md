# Analysis outputs

`outputs/` contains the frozen statistical artifacts generated by the final analysis.

- `figures/` — 15 PDF statistical/result figures.
- `tables/` — 44 CSV statistical, audit, and supporting tables.
- `supporting/` — historical execution manifest and technical-freeze notes.

## Historical naming

Some generated output filenames or fields retain the historical `EAAI` development label. They are intentionally preserved as frozen analysis artifacts rather than silently edited after the technical freeze. Current repository paths and publication-facing mappings are provided under `metadata/` and `docs/ARTIFACT_MAP.md`.

## Explanatory manuscript diagrams

Journal-facing explanatory diagrams are stored separately under `docs/figures/`. They are documentation/manuscript figures rather than frozen statistical outputs.
